What it is

The Elektron Digitakt, released in 2017, is a compact eight-track digital sampler and drum machine built around Elektron's celebrated step sequencer with parameter locks, conditional trigs, and per-step modulation. It's less about vast sampling and more about deep, performable sequencing — which is why it became a modern staple for beat-making and live sets. Elektron released the expanded Digitakt II in 2024 (more tracks, stereo sampling, more voices), so the name now covers two generations.

Buying one used — what to check

This is a current, road-tested instrument, so used buying is low-risk — the key is which generation you're getting: the original Digitakt and the Digitakt II differ meaningfully in tracks, sampling, and price, and titles sometimes just say Digitakt. On any unit, check the encoders and buttons for wear (they see heavy sequencing use), confirm the screen is clean and bright, and make sure it powers up and holds projects. Elektron keeps firmware current, so it's worth confirming the unit updates normally and includes its USB and power cabling.

Regional sourcing notes

Elektron is Swedish, not Japanese, so the domestic-Japan pricing advantage is smaller here than for Roland or Korg gear — but Digitakts still circulate widely on Yahoo Auctions, Mercari, and through Japanese shops, and the global used market for them is active. Voltage is rarely an issue since the power supply is a standard external adapter. In Japanese listings search Elektron and Digitakt, and read condition terms — 動作確認済み (tested working), 整備済み (serviced), 美品 (excellent), against ジャンク (junk) and 未確認 (untested).
